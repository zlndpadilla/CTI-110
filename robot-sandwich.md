# Robot makes a PB&J

## Assumptions

- The robot is fully charged and is constantly getting substanstial and reliable power for every task
- Both of the robot's hands are empty and open and resting on the sides of the robot
- The robot is positioned behind a table that contains:
    - An open jelly jar on the left-most end of the table
    - A plate in the center of the table with a butter knife resting flat on top of the edges of the plate
    - Two identical individual square slices of bread (no ends of a loaf and appropriately sized) stacked on top of each other to the right of the plate(both are slightly hanging off the table and the top one is slightly hanging off the bottom one not enough so it falls just enough to make it easy to grab)
    - An open peanut butter jar in between the plate and peanut butter jar
    - A napkin to the right of the bread (partly hanging off the edge of the table without falling)
- The robot is facing the plate and directly centered with it
- The robot has fully functioning robotic average human hands that it is in full control of
- The robot can reach each item and every part of each surface without any complications or additional movements, just the arms and hands


<br>

## Steps to Follow

1. Identify the butter knife and it's handle on the plate, and grab it by it's handle with left hand and if needed adjust the direction of the blade to face away from the robot, continue holding
2. Identify the top slice of the stack of bread and move right hand (palm up) underneath the bottom of the top slice without moving the bottom slice (if it exists), lightly grab the slice and place it on the center of the plate, let go of the bread
3. Move right hand (widely cupped and vertical) to the right side of the peanut butter jar and make sure the right hand is fully around the jar (without moving it) and firmly grip it, position the peanut butter jar (opening up) in between the plate and the robot (without touching either), tilt the peanut butter jar 45 degrees towards the robot
4. With the butter knife in the left hand, insert the tip of the blade into the opening of the jar, as left-most as possible and continue inserting in the same orientation until it reaches the beginning of the handle, move the butter knife from left to right in a scooping upward motion and take the blade out from the opening
5. Return the peanut butter jar to it's original position and orientation and let go
6. Move the butter knife with left hand to hover over the center of the slice of bread on the plate, rotate hand so that the peanut butter on the knife is facing the top of the slice on the plate, move the knife down so the peanut butter is grazing the bread (ensure the peanut butter is constantly grazing the respective slice until task is finished), move the knife in circular motions around the area of the top slice of bread on the plate until it is evenly spread
7. Slightly distance the knife from the plate upwards, with right hand (palm up and flat) position it underneath the bottom part of the napkin that is hanging off, carefully move right hand upwards until it touches the bottom of the napkin and carefully tilt fingertips upwards so the napkin falls into the center of the robot's right hand (the napkin should end at the end of the robot's hand), stop tilting when accomplished and lay hand flat.
8. Without dropping the napkin and keeping the right hand as is, position napkin so that the bottom of the napkin it is touching the bottom of the beginning of the handle of the knife, close robot's hand firmly so that the napkin is wrapped around the blade, move the napkin from the handle side to the blade side so that all the remaining peanut butter is taken off.
9. Move napkin away from the knife and crumple it into a ball, return the original position and drop it there.
10. Repeat steps 3-6 but substitue the peanut butter and it's jar with the jelly and it's jar
11. Repeat step 2
12. Place the knife flat on the table to the right of the plate and to the left of the napkin and let go, return hands to default position

## Done When

- A peanut butter and jelly sandwich is constructed on the plate and each item is in it's specified position
